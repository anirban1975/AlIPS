## Hard rules — never break these

These are non-negotiable. Claude must follow them in every piece of work.

1. **Never publish a student's face, full name, or class together** in any
   public marketing material without written parental consent on file.
2. **Never invent a statistic, result, ranking, testimonial, or award.**
   If a number is needed and I have not supplied it, leave a clearly
   marked `[NEED: ...]` blank instead of guessing.
3. **Never quote fees** in marketing material — direct enquiries to the
   admissions desk.
4. **Never send anything to parents, post publicly, or email externally
   without showing me the draft first.** Draft, then ask.
5. **Never make comparative claims** about other schools ("the best in the
   city", "better than X") — these invite trouble and are unverifiable.
